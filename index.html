How it works

Name a topic

Anything at all: Norse mythology, bread baking, game theory, how planes fly. Or start one of the ready-made collections.

Get a path, not a pile

Rabbithole maps it into short notes in the order you would want to learn them, and writes more as you go.

Five minutes a day

Read the next note, flip a few flashcards, answer a quick quiz. Small enough to keep doing.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Starting collections, reading notes, flashcards, quizzes and streaks are all free, with generous daily limits. A Pro plan with extras is on its way.

How are the notes made?

Rabbithole builds a learning plan for your topic, then writes each note to the same shape: a clear explanation, the key points, and questions to check yourself. Pro adds sources: real pages, quoted, that back up what you read.

How much time does it take?

About five minutes a day. One note is a 3 to 5 minute read, and the daily flashcards and quiz take a minute or two each.

Do I need to install an app?

No. It runs in the browser on any phone or laptop. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, with a daily reminder if you want one.

Can I learn more than one thing?

Yes. Keep several collections going at once, and your streak counts any of them.
