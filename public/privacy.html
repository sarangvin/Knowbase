Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Rabbithole helps you learn one fascinating thing a day. In short:

  • When you sign in with Google, we get your name, email and profile picture. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • We store what you do in the app, like your topics, notes, flashcards and streaks, so it syncs across your devices.
  • AI features send the text they need, such as your topic, to an AI provider to generate your notes.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.
  • To get a copy of your data or delete your account, email sarangvin42@gmail.com.
Learn more

Everything we collect

  • Usage records tied to your account, such as logins, note reviews and AI requests, to run the app, enforce plan limits and fix problems.
  • Anonymous counters, like hourly page views and link clicks. These don't identify anyone and are deleted after 30 days.
  • Reminders. If you turn them on, your browser's push subscription.
  • Payments. If you pay, your plan and its status. Razorpay handles the payment; we never see your card.
  • Your own AI key, if you add one. It's stored encrypted.
  • IP addresses, to rate-limit requests and prevent abuse.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in and a short-lived one completes Google sign-in. No advertising cookies.

Who else handles your data

Google (sign-in and the Gemini API), Anthropic (AI on paid plans), any AI provider whose key you add, Razorpay (payments), Vercel and Neon (hosting and database), and your browser's push service (reminders). We also share data if the law requires it.

YouTube

We use YouTube API Services only to upload videos to Rabbithole's own channel and read its analytics. The app never connects to your YouTube account. See the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

Account data stays until you ask us to delete it. You can turn off reminders in the app or your browser at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
