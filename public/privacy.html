Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

Rabbithole helps you learn one fascinating thing a day. This page explains what the app collects, why, who else handles it, and how to have it deleted. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your email address, name, profile picture, and whether Google has verified your email. We ask for nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you do in the app. The topics you pick, your notes, flashcards, quiz results, streaks, and settings.
  • Usage records tied to your account. Events such as logins, note reviews, and AI requests, including which model was used and how much it was used. We use these to run the app, enforce plan limits, and fix problems.
  • Anonymous counters. Hourly totals of steps such as landing page views and sign-up clicks. These are numbers only and don't identify anyone.
  • Notification details. If you turn on reminders, your browser's push subscription, so we can reach that device.
  • Subscription details. If you pay for a plan, your plan and subscription status. Razorpay handles the payment, and we don't store your card details.
  • Your own AI key. If you add one, it is encrypted before it is stored and is only decrypted to make your requests.
  • IP addresses. We read them to limit how many requests a single address can make, which protects the service from abuse.

Cookies

We use a cookie to keep you signed in and a short-lived one to complete Google sign-in securely. We don't use advertising cookies.

Who else handles your data

  • Google for sign-in, and Google's Gemini API for generating learning content.
  • Anthropic for generating learning content on paid plans.
  • The AI provider you choose, if you add your own key.
  • Razorpay for payments.
  • Vercel and Neon for hosting the app and its database.
  • Your browser's push service for delivering reminders.

When you use an AI feature, the text needed for that request, such as your topic or note, is sent to the AI provider. We also share data if the law requires it.

YouTube

We use YouTube API Services to upload videos to Rabbithole's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's analytics. The app doesn't connect to your YouTube account or read any of your YouTube data. Those services are covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you ask us to delete it. Anonymous counters are deleted after 30 days.

Your choices

You can turn off reminders in the app or your browser. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account and everything tied to it, email sarangvin42@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
